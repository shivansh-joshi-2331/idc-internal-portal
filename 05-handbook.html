Welcome to IDC

Welcome to IDC — we're genuinely glad you're here. This handbook is your guide to how we work, what we value, and the things that keep us running smoothly as a team.

We're a small creative agency, which means everyone here has real impact. Your work shows up in client campaigns, in the quality of our output, and in the culture we build together every day.

📌 If anything in this handbook is unclear or outdated, flag it to Jordan in Slack (#ops) and we'll update it. This is a living document.

This handbook covers everything from how we communicate day-to-day, to what tools we use, to policies around time off and expenses. Read it once when you start, then reference it as needed.

⭐
Our values

These aren't poster words — they're things we actually try to live by.

  • Do great work, honestly. Quality matters. So does owning your mistakes early.
  • Clients come second. Team comes first. A healthy team produces better client work.
  • Say the thing. We prefer honest, direct feedback to polite vagueness.
  • Own your craft. Stay curious. Keep learning. Bring your full expertise to every brief.
  • Make it fun. Friday Games exist for a reason. Enjoy the work.
✅
Onboarding checklist

Complete these in your first week. Jordan will check in with you on day 5.

Day 1 — Accounts & access ›
Set up Google Workspace, Slack, Asana, Figma, and this portal. Get added to all relevant Slack channels by Jordan. Receive your Upwork invite if applicable.
Day 2 — Meet the team ›
30-min intro calls with each team member. Calendar invites sent by Jordan. No agenda — just get to know each other.
Day 3 — Client overview ›
Review active client briefs and Asana projects. Sit in on one client call (observer only). Ask Haley for the client context doc.
Week 1 — First deliverable ›
Pick up a starter task from Asana (tagged "good first task"). Complete and submit for review by end of week. All good first tasks are low-stakes.

Communication

We use Slack for async and Zoom for sync. Here's the channel structure:

#general — Team announcements ›
Company-wide updates, big announcements, and things everyone should know. Posted by ops and leadership. Please keep it signal, not noise.
#wins — Client & team wins ›
Share something that went well. Client compliment? Great deliverable? Closed a lead? Post it here. +15 leaderboard points per post.
#creative-feedback — Work in progress ›
Post work that needs eyes. Be specific about what kind of feedback you want. Review requests must link to a Figma, doc, or video — not a screenshot.
#learning — Resources & finds ›
Articles, tools, courses, techniques. Share what you're learning. +10 leaderboard points per share. Quality over quantity.
#random — Everything else ›
Memes, recommendations, weekend plans, Friday Game banter. Be a human here.
Slack response expectations: within 2 hours during core hours for DMs. Channel messages within same business day.

Time off & PTO

Full-time employees receive 20 days PTO per year, plus public holidays in your region. PTO does not roll over.

  • Request PTO in the schedule portal at least 5 business days in advance
  • For absences over 5 days, request 2 weeks in advance
  • Jordan will approve and add it to the team calendar
  • Sick days are separate and unlimited — just let Jordan know by 9am
IDC closes for 2 weeks over Christmas/New Year (Dec 24 – Jan 2). These days are not deducted from your PTO.
